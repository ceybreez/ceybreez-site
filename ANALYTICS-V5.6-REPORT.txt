CEYBREEZ ANALYTICS V5.6
========================

GA4 Measurement ID: G-3QG71CWHW2

PUBLIC EVENTS
- inquiry_submit: fires only after the inquiry API succeeds and the common success sheet opens.
- generate_lead: GA4 recommended lead event, fired together with inquiry_submit.
- whatsapp_click: any public WhatsApp link clicked.
- contact_click: mailto/tel links, with contact_method=email or phone.
- tour_inquiry_click: a tour inquiry form submit attempt.

BUSINESS CONVERSION EVENTS
- quote_confirmed: fires from the Worker confirmation page only the first time a guest confirms a quote.
- booking_confirmed: fires when Admin successfully creates a new booking. Admin page_view tracking remains disabled.

PRIVACY
No guest name, email, phone, message, or inquiry reference is sent by the V5.6 analytics layer.

GA4 NEXT STEP
After the events appear in GA4, mark inquiry_submit, quote_confirmed and booking_confirmed as Key events.
You may also mark whatsapp_click if WhatsApp leads are commercially important.
